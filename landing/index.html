The problem

The phone rings while everyone is under a car.

Independent shops run lean. The people who could answer are the people turning wrenches, and a caller with a check-engine light does not leave a voicemail.

10:42 am

Your tech is mid brake job.

Gloves on, wheel off. The phone rings out on the counter.

10:43 am

The caller tries the next shop.

They searched "auto repair near me". You were first on the list. Now you're not.

10:44 am

That job is gone.

Nobody calls back a voicemail they never left. You never even learn it happened.

How it works

Built first. Emailed second. Yours if you like it.

01

We build your receptionist

Before we contact you, we read your public listing and website and set up a receptionist that knows your hours, services and how you book work.

02

You call it

We email you its number. Call it like a customer. Try to stump it. Nothing is connected to your real line yet.

03

Turn it on for a week

Like it? Forward your missed calls to it. The first week is free and needs no card. Every call is reviewed and the receptionist keeps getting better.
